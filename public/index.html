Why this is more than a chatbot

1. Persistent profile

The family enters structured information once and the system reuses it across modules.

2. Decision engine

Rules combine needs, trends, status and opportunities instead of only generating conversation.

3. Proactive action

The system ranks what to do next, tracks pending items and generates alerts.

Citizen & Family Profile

One structured household profile powering eligibility matching, alerts, finance, education, livelihood and citizen-service recommendations.

Profile designKeep the profile focused on information needed for analysis and service matching. Never enter passwords, PINs, OTPs, full bank-account numbers or other secrets.

Family members

A non-official demonstration score showing where the family can improve.

How the score works

  • Financial resilience and trend
  • Education and skill development
  • Government-service completion
  • Agriculture/livelihood stability
  • Health organization
  • Documents and household readiness

This is a project metric, not an official government rating and not a measure of a person's worth.
